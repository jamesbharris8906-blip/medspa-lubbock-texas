What a first-timer should know

First, you do not need to know the names of the treatments. You only need to know what you see. Dry skin? Lines? Spots? Hair? Thin hair? Tell your provider. They will match the issue to a treatment. Second, most visits are short. Many take less than an hour. Third, you can ask about pain, cost, and heal time before you pick. A good provider wants those questions. Fourth, one visit is often not the end. Skin care works over time. A plan with a few visits often works best.

How to pick a treatment

Think in small steps. Pick one issue to start with. If lines bother you most, Botox may be first. If spots bother you most, a laser may be first. If hair bothers you most, laser hair removal may be first. If dry skin bothers you most, skin boosters may be first. You can add a second step later. A Medspa Lubbock Texas plan does not need to be big to work. Small steps, done well, can add up to a clear change.

Ask about time and cost for each choice. Ask how many visits it takes. Ask what your skin will look like the next day. Ask what to skip after. Write the answers down. Then pick the one that fits your week. A plan that fits your life is a plan you can keep.

Services at K-Glow

Below are the main treatments at K-Glow. Each one has a job. Each one works best for certain skin. Read them with your own goal in mind. Your provider will help you pick the right mix.

Botox

Botox lets a small face muscle rest for a while. Lines that come from frowns and squints can look soft. The forehead, the brow, and the eye corners are the most common spots. The shots are fast and the pinch is light. Most people go back to their day right after. A Medspa Lubbock Texas Botox visit can be a good first step for many people. The change builds over days.

Dermal Fillers

Fillers are a gel that sits under the skin. They can bring back volume in cheeks and lips. They can soften the lines near the mouth. The change is seen at once in the mirror. A bit of puffiness can show for a day. Your provider will shape it with you and check that both sides look even.

XERF

XERF warms the skin from below to help it feel tight. No cuts are made. The visit is calm and the heal time is short. Over weeks, the skin can feel firmer on the face and neck. It suits people who want a lift without a needle in that spot. Ask if it fits your skin type.

LaseMD Ultra

LaseMD Ultra is a light treatment that helps old skin make way for new skin. It can help dark spots from the sun. It can help rough skin feel smooth. It can help tone look even. Pink skin for a day is common. After that, many people see a fresh look. Sun care after is a must.

Potenza RF Microneedling

Potenza uses fine needles plus heat. The needles make tiny spots. The heat helps the skin build collagen. That can help acne scars, big pores, and lines. It can help skin that has lost its firm feel. The skin may be red for a day or two. The best change comes over weeks.

PicoSure Pro

PicoSure Pro is a laser with very fast light. It can break up dark spots and old scars. It can help skin tone look more even. It can fade some tattoos. The visit is fast and the feel is a light snap. You may need more than one visit. Your provider will set a fair plan.

Keralase

Keralase is for the scalp and hair. A laser makes small paths on the scalp. A serum is put on those paths. The aim is hair that looks fuller over a set of visits. It is not a fast fix. Your provider will look at your scalp and tell you the time it may take.

Titanium Laser Hair Removal

This laser works on hair at the root. It can be used on the legs, arms, underarms, and bikini line. It can be used on the face for some people. You will need a set of visits, as hair grows in rounds. Shave before each visit. Skip waxing and plucking in the weeks before.

Skin Boosters

Skin Boosters are small shots that help skin hold water. Dry skin can look soft and dewy after. Fine lines can look less sharp. They do not add big volume. They add a light glow. They are used on the face, neck, and hands. Small bumps after can fade in a day.

After your visit

After a treatment, go easy on your skin. Use the cream your provider gives you. Wear sun care each day. Drink water. Skip strong scrubs for a few days. If your skin feels sore or looks odd, call us. Do not try to fix it on your own. A quick call can save a lot of worry.

Questions about medspa visits

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring a list of your medicines. Bring the skin items you use now. Bring a photo of a look you like, if you have one. That helps your provider see your goal.

Will it hurt?

Most treatments feel like a pinch or a warm snap. Some feel like almost nothing. Your provider will tell you what to expect. A cold pack or numbing cream can help for some treatments.

How soon can I wear makeup?

It depends on the treatment. Some let you wear it the same day. Some ask you to wait a day. Ask your provider before you leave. They will give you a clear time.

Can I work out after?

Some treatments ask you to skip hard workouts for a day. Some do not. Sweat can sting fresh skin. Ask for your treatment and plan your gym day around it.

Do treatments work for all skin tones?

Many do, but not all. Your provider will check your skin tone and pick a safe choice. Tell them about your skin and any past marks or scars.

How do I care for my skin at home?

Use a soft wash, a plain cream, and sun care each day. Skip strong acids for a few days after a treatment. Drink water. Your provider can help you pick a simple home plan.

When should I book again?

Your provider will tell you a time to come back. Botox is often in months. Lasers and microneedling are often in weeks. Call (806) 805-1530 when you are ready to book the next step.
